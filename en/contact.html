03 Before you write

Four things that make your enquiry much easier to answer.

  • 01

    The problem, not the specification

    “Our booking page loses people at the date picker” tells us far more than “we need a new website with a modern design”. We can work out the solution; only you know the symptom.

  • 02

    A real deadline, if you have one

    A trade show, a funding round, a product launch, a contract ending. Deadlines shape scope more than budget does, and knowing yours up front prevents a proposal that cannot land in time.

  • 03

    An honest budget range

    Not a negotiating position — a range. It lets us propose something that can actually be built, or tell you straight away that we are not the right fit and point you somewhere better.

  • 04

    Who signs off

    One decision-maker keeps a project on schedule; a committee of six does not. Telling us the approval chain early lets us build the right number of review rounds into the plan.
