Ask about care

Speed, specifically

Fast is not a finishing touch. It is the build method.

We set a page-weight budget during design and hold every decision against it. Fonts are subset, images are sized and lazy-loaded, JavaScript is written rather than installed, and the animation layer is profiled on real hardware.

When we take on an optimisation engagement for a site we did not build, you get a prioritised list with the estimated cost and expected gain of each fix, so you can decide what is worth doing before we start.

Performance service

Commerce, specifically

We design the checkout first, then the rest of the store.

The stretch between a product page and a completed payment is where stores lose the most money and get the least design attention. We start there — variant selection, delivery clarity, guest checkout, the exact moment a shipping cost appears — then build the catalogue to feed it.

Event tracking goes in from the first commit, so within a fortnight of launch you can see precisely which step people abandon.

06 Questions

The things people ask first.

A custom marketing site of eight to twelve pages generally lands in the mid five figures; commerce and multi-language builds run higher. We quote a fixed price after a scoping call and a written brief, so the number you approve is the number you pay. If your budget is below the range, say so in your enquiry — we will tell you honestly whether we can help or recommend someone who can.

Eight weeks is typical for a full custom build, from kickoff to launch. Commerce projects and sites needing several languages run ten to twelve. The single biggest variable is how quickly content and feedback come back from your side, which is why the schedule names your deadlines as clearly as ours.

You provide the raw material and the subject-matter expertise. We structure it, edit it for the web, and write connective copy where it is missing. If you need full copywriting, we bring in a writer we have worked with for years and fold the cost into the quote up front.

Yes, and we design for it. CMS builds ship with purpose-made blocks that cannot be arranged into something broken, plus a written handbook and a recorded walkthrough for your team. Most clients are editing confidently within a day.

Most of our work is remote, across the mainland United States, the wider Caribbean and Europe. We keep a two-hour overlap with your working day and run scheduled check-ins rather than ad-hoc calls, which tends to suit distributed teams better anyway.

Thirty days of monitoring and fixes are included with every build. After that you can take the site entirely in-house — you own everything — or move onto a care plan for updates, security and a quarterly review of how the site is actually performing.
