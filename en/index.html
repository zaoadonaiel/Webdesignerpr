01 The studio

A website is the only employee that works every hour of the year. Ours are trained properly.

WebDesignerPR is an independent studio in San Juan. We work with founders, marketing leads and operators who have outgrown the site they started with — and who know that the next one has to do real work.

Everything is built from a blank file. No purchased themes, no page builders, no fifteen plugins holding the layout together. That takes longer up front and it is the reason our sites are still fast three years later.

We are deliberately small. The person who designs your site is the person who builds it and the person who answers your email in year two.

04 Why choose us

The part most studios leave out.

  • 01

    One team, from first call to year three

    No account manager relaying messages to a designer who relays them to a developer. You talk to the people doing the work, which is why briefs survive contact with production.

  • 02

    Performance is a design constraint

    We set a page-weight budget in week one and design inside it. Animation, imagery and type all get measured against it. A beautiful site that takes six seconds to load is a broken site.

  • 03

    You own every file

    Code, design source, domain, hosting, analytics — all in your accounts, in your name. There is no proprietary platform holding your site hostage and no exit fee.

  • 04

    Built to be edited by humans

    Your team will need to change things without calling us. Every CMS build ships with a written handbook and a walkthrough recording, so the site stays current after we hand over the keys.

05 How we work

Six stages. Eight weeks, typically.

Every stage ends with something you can see and approve, so there is never a month where you wonder what is happening.

01

Discovery

We learn your business before we open a design tool. Goals, margins, competitors, the objections your sales team hears every week. You leave this stage with a written brief you could hand to any studio.

Week 1

02

Architecture

Sitemap, page-by-page content map, and the single decision each page exists to support. Structure is settled here, in cheap grey boxes, rather than three weeks later in expensive finished design.

Week 1–2

03

Design

Direction first, then full pages with your real copy. Two rounds of revision are built into the schedule, and you sign off on a prototype you can click through on your own phone.

Week 2–4

04

Build

Hand-written front-end, CMS integration, and the animation layer tuned against a performance budget. You get a staging link from the first week of build and watch it come together.

Week 4–7

05

Launch

Cross-browser QA, accessibility pass, redirect map, analytics, sitemap and a rehearsed cutover. We launch mid-week, in the morning, with everyone available — never on a Friday afternoon.

Week 7–8

06

Iterate

Thirty days of monitoring included. After that we look at the numbers together each quarter and decide what to change — a website is an asset to maintain, not a project to finish.
